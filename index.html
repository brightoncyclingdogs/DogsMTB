Who We Are

We are a team focused on student athletes riding and racing mountain bikes within the Michigan Scholastic Cycling Association (MiSCA), supported by parent coaches.

Our team is made up of student athletes, K-12, in Brighton and from the surrounding communities.

We enjoy riding bikes on fun dirt trails through the woods, over rocks, and roots!
